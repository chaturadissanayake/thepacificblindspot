Methodology and sources

This data story relies exclusively on verified open data from regional authorities, the UN Systematic Observations Financing Facility (SOFF), the World Meteorological Organisation (WMO), the Pacific Data Hub (SPC), and World Bank Climate Risk Country Profiles. Full access to raw data files and processing scripts is provided below.

Data sources

  • Kiribati SOFF Investment Phase Funding Request UN SOFF
  • Directly Affected Persons by Disasters Pacific Data Hub
  • Direct Disaster Economic Loss, Average Annual Loss Pacific Data Hub
  • Meteorological Monitoring Network, Land Stations Pacific Data Hub
  • Climate Risk Country Profiles & Sea Level Projections World Bank CCKP
  • Historical High Tide Flooding Days NOAA
  • SOFF Portfolio and Implementation Progress UN SOFF
  • WMO GBON Global Baseline Diagnostic Report World Meteorological Organisation
  • Terrain Imagery & Country Maps Google Earth & Google Maps

About the project

This data story was created for the Pacific Dataviz Challenge 2026 to expose the critical lack of meteorological tracking infrastructure in the world's most climate-vulnerable region.
