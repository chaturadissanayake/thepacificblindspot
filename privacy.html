Privacy Policy

How data is handled on this interactive project.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Analytics and cookies

The Pacific Blind Spot uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which chapters people read. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser and approximate location. The data is aggregated, and I do not use it for advertising or profiling. To opt out, block the script in your browser or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. There are no advertising pixels on this site.

2. Server logs

The hosting provider automatically collects basic, anonymised server logs (which may include your IP address, browser type and timestamp) for security and to keep the site online. This data is not used for profiling or marketing.

3. External links

This site contains links to the Pacific Data Hub and other external resources for citation purposes. Once you click a link and leave this site, you are governed by the privacy policies and data collection practices of those specific platforms.

4. Open source

The code for this project is open-source. Any interactions you have with the underlying repository (e.g., GitHub) are governed by that platform's respective privacy policy.
